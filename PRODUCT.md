# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers on a Mac who ship to Vercel and Cloudflare. They open DeployDeck at a desk, often beside an editor, to check a build, roll something back, or fix a DNS record without opening two browser dashboards.

## Product Purpose

DeployDeck is a local deployment console. Success is a developer finishing a normal deploy task (inspect, retry, promote, edit DNS, change an env var) without leaving the app or re-entering tokens.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, nocturnal. Like a hardware instrument on a night desk: graphite chassis, one warm indicator light, no ceremony.

## Anti-references

- Marketing SaaS dashboards with giant metric cards and uptime theater
- Website-in-Electron chrome (sticky marketing nav, glass stacks, gradient blobs)
- Neon cyberpunk / AI-purple tool skins
- Multi-page onboarding carousels that delay first value

## Design Principles

- The tool disappears into the task.
- Density over decoration. Tables and filters are the product.
- One accent, used rarely. Status color carries meaning, not brand.
- First-run setup is real work (connect, choose prefs), not a tour of features.
- Motion clarifies state changes. It never makes the user wait to start working.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA for body and controls. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with fade-only or instant transitions. System fonts. Keyboard paths for navigation, search, and the command palette. Placeholder and muted text must stay readable.
